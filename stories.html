How We Handle Stories

Your story stays yours.

You choose what is shared

You decide how much to tell and what stays private.

You choose your name

Stories can be published with your name, first name only, or fully anonymously.

Nothing is published without permission

We confirm with you before anything appears on this website.

You can withdraw

Ask us to remove your story at any time and we will take it down.
