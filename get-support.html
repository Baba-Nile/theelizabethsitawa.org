What We Offer

Cancer Support Services.

As a locally rooted, volunteer-led organization, what we can offer grows as our resources do. Every service below is available to reach out about. Select a service to see who it's for and how to request it.

01

Awareness, Detection & Navigation

Helping people understand cancer, catch it early, and find their way through the health system.

Who qualifies: Open to everyone: individuals, families, schools, churches and workplaces in Trans Nzoia County.
What's available: Talks, printed material and one-on-one conversations on cancer prevention, symptoms and treatment.
Where: Community venues, schools, churches and workplaces across Trans Nzoia County.
How to apply: Contact us to request a talk or session for your group.
Documents needed: None.
Contact: Sitawa Kisiangani · +254 798 410193 or elizabethsitawacbo@gmail.com
Who qualifies: Community members, with a priority on people with limited access to routine screening.
What's available: Awareness on warning signs, and help connecting to facilities that offer screening.
Where: Community outreach events, and by referral to partner health facilities.
How to apply: Attend an announced screening event, or contact us to ask about the next one.
Documents needed: None to attend; the screening facility itself may ask for identification.
Contact: Sitawa Kisiangani · +254 798 410193 or elizabethsitawacbo@gmail.com
Who qualifies: Anyone newly diagnosed or in treatment who needs help understanding next steps.
What's available: Help understanding a diagnosis, finding the right facility or specialist, and navigating the health system.
Where: By phone, in person in Kitale, or via referral to a healthcare facility.
How to apply: Submit a support request below, or call us directly.
Documents needed: Sharing diagnosis details helps us guide you, but isn't required to reach out.
Contact: Sitawa Kisiangani · +254 798 410193 or elizabethsitawacbo@gmail.com
Who qualifies: Patients and families needing palliative or end-of-life care support.
What's available: Help identifying and connecting to palliative-care providers. We do not provide clinical care ourselves.
Where: Referral to healthcare facilities.
How to apply: Contact us and we'll help identify appropriate care options.
Documents needed: None required to ask.
Contact: Sitawa Kisiangani · +254 798 410193 or elizabethsitawacbo@gmail.com
02

Emotional & Community Support

A steady presence for patients, survivors, caregivers and families throughout the journey.

Who qualifies: Patients, survivors, caregivers and family members.
What's available: A listening ear, one-on-one conversations, and connection to our wider support community.
Where: In person in Kitale, or by phone.
How to apply: Use the request form below, or call or email us.
Documents needed: None.
Contact: Sitawa Kisiangani · +254 798 410193 or elizabethsitawacbo@gmail.com
Who qualifies: Cancer survivors in Trans Nzoia County.
What's available: A space to connect with others who have completed treatment.
Where: Community gatherings, announced through our Facebook group.
How to apply: Let us know you'd like to join, and we'll notify you of the next gathering.
Documents needed: None.
Contact: Sitawa Kisiangani · +254 798 410193 or elizabethsitawacbo@gmail.com
Who qualifies: Anyone caring for a person with cancer.
What's available: Emotional support, practical guidance, and connection to other caregivers.
Where: In person or by phone.
How to apply: Use the caregiver support form further down this page.
Documents needed: None.
Contact: Sitawa Kisiangani · +254 798 410193 or elizabethsitawacbo@gmail.com
Who qualifies: Patients unable to travel, or those in hospital who would welcome a visit.
What's available: Companionship and practical support visits.
Where: Homes and hospitals within Trans Nzoia County.
How to apply: Request a visit through the form below or by phone.
Documents needed: None.
Contact: Sitawa Kisiangani · +254 798 410193 or elizabethsitawacbo@gmail.com
Who qualifies: Survivors, patients and volunteers within our community.
What's available: Recognition and encouragement for people navigating, or supporting others through, cancer.
Where: Community gatherings and events.
How to apply: Nominate someone, or contact us to learn more.
Documents needed: None.
Contact: Sitawa Kisiangani · +254 798 410193 or elizabethsitawacbo@gmail.com
03

Practical & Financial Assistance

Removing the everyday barriers that stand between a patient and their treatment.

Who qualifies: Patients facing difficulty reaching treatment or screening appointments, assessed by need and available resources.
What's available: Help arranging or contributing toward transport to appointments, where resources allow.
Where: Within Trans Nzoia County.
How to apply: Contact us ahead of your appointment so we can discuss what's possible.
Documents needed: Your appointment date and facility help us plan.
Contact: Sitawa Kisiangani · +254 798 410193 or elizabethsitawacbo@gmail.com
Who qualifies: Patients and families facing food insecurity during treatment, based on need and available resources.
What's available: Practical nutrition assistance where resources allow.
Where: Trans Nzoia County.
How to apply: Reach out and tell us your situation.
Documents needed: None required to ask.
Contact: Sitawa Kisiangani · +254 798 410193 or elizabethsitawacbo@gmail.com
Who qualifies: Patients facing financial barriers to treatment, assessed case by case as resources allow.
What's available: Assistance where we can provide it, plus connections to other sources of support.
Where: Trans Nzoia County.
How to apply: Contact us to discuss your situation. We'll let you know what's possible.
Documents needed: We'll advise what's needed once we understand your situation.
Contact: Sitawa Kisiangani · +254 798 410193 or elizabethsitawacbo@gmail.com
