01 Who We Are

A community built around courage, dignity and hope.

The Elizabeth Sitawa Cancer Community Based Organization is an independent, non-political, voluntary community-based organization working in Trans Nzoia County, Kenya. We raise cancer awareness, fight stigma, advocate for people affected by cancer, and mobilize support for individuals and families facing the realities of a diagnosis.

What We Do

Support that meets people where they are.

Our work is organized around five areas drawn directly from our constitution, from first awareness to standing beside a family through diagnosis and treatment.

Cancer Awareness

Building community knowledge around cancer, risk factors, screening and early detection.

Fighting Stigma

Challenging stigma, misinformation and discrimination surrounding a cancer diagnosis.

Patient & Family Support

Standing with people living with cancer and the families navigating its impact.

Patient Rights

Advocating for the rights and dignity of people living with and affected by cancer.

Partnerships

Working with government, NGOs, healthcare organizations and development partners.
