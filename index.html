Who We Are

A community built around courage, dignity and hope.

Elizabeth Sitawa Cancer C.B.O. is an independent, non-political, voluntary community-based organization working in Trans Nzoia County, Kenya. We raise cancer awareness, fight stigma, advocate for people affected by cancer, and mobilize support for individuals and families facing the realities of a diagnosis.

Elizabeth's Journey

One journey. One voice. A reason to speak.

Elizabeth Sitawa's own cancer journey is the reason this organization exists. Her diagnosis, treatment and recovery convinced her that no one should walk this path in silence — and that speaking openly about cancer can change how a whole community responds to it.
