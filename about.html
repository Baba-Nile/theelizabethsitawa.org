Who We Are

Our story

Elizabeth Sitawa Cancer C.B.O. is a non-political, voluntary community-based organization registered and operating in Kitale Town, Trans Nzoia County, Kenya. The organization was founded to raise cancer awareness, challenge the stigma that surrounds a cancer diagnosis, advocate for the dignity of people affected by cancer, and mobilize support for individuals and families navigating the realities of the disease.

Our work is rooted in a simple conviction: cancer should not be faced in silence, and the people who face it deserve a community that sees them, supports them, and stands beside them.

Our Vision

"To have a society respecting and taking care of cancer fighters."

Our Mission

"Mobilize human, financial and material resources for societal relief and empowerment."

Our Objectives

Areas of focus guiding our work.

Cancer Awareness

Building community knowledge and understanding around cancer.

Fighting Cancer Stigma

Challenging stigma, misinformation and discrimination surrounding cancer.

Supporting Affected Families

Standing with people living with cancer and the families affected by it.

Patient Rights

Advocating for the rights and dignity of people living with and affected by cancer.

Career Awareness

Building awareness and opportunities tied to community empowerment.

Partnerships

Working with government, NGOs, healthcare organizations and approved development partners.

Our Values

What guides our work.

Dignity

Courage

Where We Work

Trans Nzoia County, Kenya.

We are based in Kitale Town and our area of operation is Trans Nzoia County, where we work directly with cancer fighters, families and community members, and alongside healthcare institutions, government offices and partner organizations.
